OPENCHURCH — INITIAL VECTOR LOGO SET

Brand relationship
- Parent company: Pillars Connect Ltd.
- Product/project: OpenChurch, a free and open-source website platform for churches across denominations.
- The OpenChurch mark is intended to be a distinct product identity. It does not reproduce or incorporate the Pillars Connect logo.

Logo concept
- The icon is an abstract geometric portal / open-frame mark.
- The deliberate lower-right opening suggests access, welcome, and openness without depicting a church, cross, book, or denomination.
- Its restrained geometric construction is intended to sit comfortably alongside a modern software brand.

Brand colours
- Primary deep green: #1B5142
- Warm off-white: #FEFDFB
- Soft cream: #F6EFE1
- Supporting dark neutral: #252B29

Files

- openchurch-icon.svg          Standalone icon, transparent background
- openchurch-monochrome.svg    Single-colour logo
- openchurch-reversed.svg      Light logo for dark backgrounds

Notes
- SVG files are editable vector artwork. The wordmark uses Inter with generic sans-serif fallbacks.
- This is an initial design direction, not a trademark clearance or final identity specification.
